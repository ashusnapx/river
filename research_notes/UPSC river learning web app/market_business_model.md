# Market Size, Willingness to Pay, Distribution and Business Models for a River-First Geography App for Indian Exam Aspirants

Research date: 18 October 2026. Every figure is dated. ₹1 crore (cr) = ₹10 million; 1 lakh = 100,000. Sources are mostly secondary (news, coaching blogs, aggregators). Official UPSC and PIB PDFs were not fetched directly. Conflicts between sources are flagged.

## 1. How many aspirants are there, and which syllabi include river geography?

### Takeaway
About 5.5 to 5.8 lakh people actually sit UPSC CSE Prelims each year. That number has held steady since 2022, while applications have fallen from about 13 lakh in 2023 to 8.19 lakh in 2026. Most of the volume is in state PSCs (each has 2.4 to 11.5 lakh appearers), SSC (28 to 37 lakh applicants per exam), defence (about 17 lakh UPSC defence applicants a year), CTET (25 lakh) and CBSE boards (43.7 lakh). All of these test geography, but it is only a small slice of each paper.

**Is geography, and rivers specifically, in the syllabus?**
- UPSC Prelims geography questions: one analysis counts 12 in 2021 and 23 in 2025; another counts 4 in 2016 and 20 in 2024 — [UPSCprep](https://www.upscprep.com/simplify-geography-using-micro-themes-for-prelims-2025/); [Anantam IAS](https://anantamias.com/?p=43792).

A 2024 question asked about rivers draining into the Red Sea — [PW](https://www.pw.live/upsc/exams/upsc-map-based-pyq); [UnderstandUPSC](https://www.understandupsc.com/geography-for-upsc-prelims-2025-important-topics-sources-trends-and-tips/).

### Inferences
- **Serious UPSC pool.** Applications fell 39% from 2023 to 2026 (13.4 lakh to 8.19 lakh), while appearers stayed between 5.5 and 5.8 lakh. The drop is in casual applicants, and the hard-core pool is stable. A reasonable proxy for serious UPSC aspirants is the roughly 5.5 lakh who appear each year. A tighter proxy is people who score near the cutoff, roughly 1 to 2 lakh, but no source quantifies this.
- **Volume is outside UPSC.** Per-cycle appearers across the big state PSCs (UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, TN Group 4) alone add roughly 22 lakh, and SSC adds another roughly 60 lakh applications.
- **Weightage falls as volume rises.** In UPSC, geography is 10–20% of Prelims and rivers are a sub-slice. In SSC and defence papers, geography is about 2–7 questions. Rivers alone justify a feature or module, not a full subscription, in SSC and defence. State PSCs add state-specific river geography (for example, rivers of Rajasthan or Bihar), which is a localisation opportunity. This last point is my inference; state syllabi were not checked.
- **School segment.** CBSE Class 10 (25 lakh) is the largest single river-geography audience, since NCERT Class 9 and 11 have drainage chapters. This is from my own knowledge and was not verified in this research. Willingness to pay is lower there, and buying goes through parents or schools.

### Gaps
- No primary UPSC or PIB PDF was fetched. The 2024 applicant count is disputed (9.93 lakh vs 13.4 lakh), and the 2023 appeared count is missing.
- No reliable counts of aspirants in Delhi (Mukherjee Nagar, Old Rajinder Nagar, Karol Bagh) or Prayagraj.
- No data on how many aspirants are repeaters.
- Missing applicant counts: MPPSC, MPSC, TNPSC Group 1, NDA and CDS for 2025 individually, CAPF 2025, CHSL 2025, RRB NTPC 2025.
- No official SSC, CAPF or state PSC syllabus text was fetched.

## 2. How big is the test-prep / government-exam edtech market, and how are the key players doing financially?

### Takeaway
Estimates of the total market range from $11.6B to $14.8B (FY25–FY26), growing 9–15% a year. There is no credible standalone figure for the government-exam or UPSC segment. Among players, only PhysicsWallah has scale, at ₹2,887 cr revenue in FY25 and listed in November 2025. Offline UPSC brands such as Drishti (₹364 cr revenue, ₹61 cr profit in FY25) are profitable. The online government-exam pure plays are all sub-scale and loss-making: Adda247 at ₹217 cr, Testbook at ₹137 cr, Unacademy at ₹826 cr and shrinking.

## 3. What will aspirants pay, and what are the ARPU and conversion benchmarks?

### Takeaway
Payment splits into two extremes. Offline UPSC foundation courses cost ₹1.55–1.95 lakh, test series ₹15,000–35,000 and geography optionals ₹30,000–50,000. Digital and app products cost ₹1,000–5,000 a year. Mapping-specific products cluster at ₹999–1,299. Mass-market online ARPU is about ₹650–3,700 a year, and paying users are 4–6% of the monthly active base.

### Cited Findings
**Offline and premium UPSC prices**
- Vajiram & Ravi 2027 GS foundation: ₹1,80,000, or ₹1,95,000 with CSAT, the same online and offline. Two-year course: ₹2,45,000 offline, ₹2,25,000 online — [Vajiram](https://vajiramandravi.com/ias-fees-structure/).
- VisionIAS Jaipur GS foundation: ₹1,55,000 (₹1,20,000 for the Hindi batch). Dates are from December 2024 — [VisionIAS Jaipur](https://visionias.in/jaipur).
- Test series: VisionIAS All-India Prelims test series about ₹15,000 (third-party figure). VisionIAS Mains 2027 test series ₹25,000–35,000 (official page) — [BharatNotes](https://bharatnotes.com/faq/mock-tests/best-prelims-test-series-2026/); [VisionIAS register](https://www.visionias.in/register).

- A vendor blog claims 75% of government-job aspirants prefer ₹199–499 monthly test series over a ₹5,999 annual plan. This is not a rigorous source — [Upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/edtech-gtm-strategy-frameworks-execution/).

### Inferences
- **Realistic price point.** A standalone river or map tool would sit at ₹299–999 a year, or ₹99–199 a month in Prelims season. That matches existing mapping modules (₹999–1,299) and Testbook Pass (₹1,499 a year).
- **Ceiling for rivers alone.** Take 5.5 lakh UPSC appearers, 5–10% paying (above the mass-market rate because these users are serious) and ₹700 average revenue. That gives about ₹2–4 cr a year. Adding state PSCs (about 22 lakh appearers, 2–4% paying, ₹400) gives about ₹2–3.5 cr. SSC and defence at 1% paying and ₹300 give about ₹2 cr. The combined river-first B2C ceiling is roughly ₹6–10 cr a year. This is a back-of-envelope estimate, not sourced.
- **Path to a large business.** Getting to ₹100 cr+ needs either (a) a full geography/GS product at about ₹3,000 ACPU across hundreds of thousands of payers (PhysicsWallah's model) or (b) B2B or licensing.

### Gaps
- No verified current prices for Unacademy Plus or Iconic, PW OnlyIAS, StudyIQ courses or the Adda247 Mahapack (only a 2021 price of ₹2,999–5,494 was found).
- No audited free-to-paid conversion data for any UPSC product.
- No Testbook subscriber counts or ARPU.

## 4. How do aspirants find products (YouTube, Telegram, Instagram, WhatsApp), and what does acquisition cost?

### Takeaway
Discovery runs on YouTube. Every major brand has 10–100M subscribers, and free YouTube content is the top of the funnel. Telegram is where notes and PDFs circulate, but it is fragile: piracy and a government block in June 2026 show the risk. Free AI and quiz tools have reached 60,000–200,000 users within a year or two. Implied paid CAC at PhysicsWallah's scale is about ₹600–1,000 per paid user.

### Cited Findings
**YouTube reach**
- PhysicsWallah: 98.8M subscribers across 207 YouTube channels (June 2025); 119.27M followers across 888 handles; 22.85B cumulative views; DAU of 2.70M (FY25) — [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/physicswallah-reports-4-13-million-paying-users-in-fy25-diversifies-across-13-education-segments-82106.htm).
- StudyIQ IAS: about 20.4M subscribers and 8.3B views (2025). Drishti IAS: 12.8M (2026 guide) — [subsub](https://open.subsub.io/channels/studyiqeducationltd/view); [EducationWorld](https://educationworld.in/5-youtube-channels-every-serious-upsc-aspirant-should-follow/).
- Adda247 (November 2023): more than 28M YouTube subscribers and 200M monthly views — [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/adda247-announces-100-yoy-revenue-growth-in-fy-2023-reporting-inr-130-cr-revenue/).

**Telegram**
- On 16–22 June 2026 the government blocked Telegram in India under Section 69A of the IT Act, on NTA advice, around the NEET-UG re-exam. Message editing was also disabled until 30 June 2026 — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/breaking-news/telegram-blocked-india-neet-paper-leak-ban); [Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/india/neet-ug-re-exam-centre-temporarily-suspends-telegram-4041576).

Petitioners argued that Telegram is a "primary archive of legitimate educational resources" — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/?p=270311); [TorrentFreak](https://torrentfreak.com/telegram-discloses-user-details-of-); [PrimeLegal](https://blog.primelegal.in/delhi-hc-upholds-telegram-ban-neet/).

**Free and AI tools as growth engines**
- SuperKalam: $2M seed (May 2024, led by Y Combinator); more than 60,000 students at the time — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=458365); [VentureIntelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/private-equity/test-prep-platform-superkalam-raises-rs.16-cr-led-by-ycombinator,-fundersclub).

The company claims its AI ranked "#1" on the 2024 Prelims paper, which is unverified — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/04/edtech-sigiqai-9-million-seed-funding-gsv-ventures-peak-xv-ai); [Entrepreneur](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/sigiqai-raises-usd-95-mn-funding-co-led-by-the-house-fund/489673).
- Global map-quiz precedent: GeoGuessr bought the free geography quiz site Seterra in 2022 and ended free-to-play in February 2024. Revenue estimates range from about $8M to $50M (Dealroom gives $10–50M and a 2025 valuation range of $500M–1B; low reliability) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GeoGuessr); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/geoguessr/); [Beehiiv](https://private-equity-bros.beehiiv.com/p/this-simple-app-was-bootstrapped-to-a-400m-valuation).

**Customer acquisition cost**
- PhysicsWallah FY25: advertising and publicity ₹276.2 cr (about 10% of revenue), or ₹448 cr total marketing as reported by Entrackr. It has earmarked ₹710 cr of IPO proceeds for promotion — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/advertising/physicswallah-ramps-up-ad-spend-to-rs-276-crore-in-fy25-earmarks-rs-710-crore-for-promotions-from-ipo-80479.htm); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/physicswallah-spent-rs-1426-cr-on-salaries-in-fy25-offline-arpu-stood-at-rs-40405-10059672).
- PhysicsWallah Q1 FY27 ad spend: ₹128 cr (+9%) — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/physicswallah-q1-results-net-loss-narrows-30-yoy-ad-spend-up-9-to-128-cr).
- Pre-COVID edtech CAC was about ₹25,000 per user (investor quote, The Ken). In FY22, Byju's, Unacademy, upGrad and Vedantu together spent ₹3,043 cr on marketing — [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/edsetgo/flipping-the-edtech-script/).

### Inferences
- **Implied CAC.** PhysicsWallah's ₹276–448 cr divided by 4.46M paid users is about ₹620–1,000 per paid user, against about ₹3,683 ACPU, so payback is under a year. A ₹500–999 river product cannot afford paid acquisition. It has to grow organically through shareable free tools (map quizzes, PYQ river maps, Shorts and Reels of river-map animations) and partnerships with YouTube educators.
- **Telegram.** It is good for distribution but should not be the only channel. Content leaks there instantly, and the June 2026 block shows platform risk. An interactive web app is naturally harder to pirate than PDFs, which is a structural advantage.
- **Free AI tools.** These grow user counts quickly (60,000–200,000), but none has disclosed revenue. Monetisation is still unproven.

### Gaps
- No survey data on what share of UPSC aspirants use YouTube, Telegram, Instagram or WhatsApp.
- No published CAC for UPSC or government-exam products specifically.
- No WhatsApp channel or Instagram follower data was collected.
- No documented case of an Indian free PYQ or quiz tool going viral and converting to revenue.

## 5. Is there precedent for a narrow tool becoming a large platform, and at what valuations?

### Takeaway
Exits for Indian exam-prep companies have been small. Single-vertical exits landed at $20–50M (StudyIQ, PrepLadder, Gradeup), and the post-2022 reset priced Unacademy at about 2–5x revenue. Only PhysicsWallah reached multi-billion scale, by going broad across 13 categories and both online and offline. Profitable offline UPSC brands were priced at about 6–7x revenue (Drishti talks, not completed).

### Cited Findings
**Deals and valuations**
- PhysicsWallah IPO (November 2025): ₹103–109 price band, about ₹31,500 cr valuation at issue; listed at ₹145 (+33%) and closed day one at ₹155, a market value of about ₹44,382 cr (about $5B). The issue was ₹3,480 cr and 1.8x subscribed. In September 2024 its Series B was $210M at $2.8B — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/ipo/physicswallah-stock-market-debut-listing-gain-125111800906_1.html); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/physicswallah-lists-at-rs-145-with-33-premium-on-stock-exchange-10784210); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=479015).

PhysicsWallah also looked at Rau's IAS, Chaitanya Academy and Sarrthi IAS, and took 40% of Sarrthi — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-physicswallah-in-talks-to-acquire-drishti-ias-for-rs-2500-cr-8907631); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-physicswallahdrishti-ias-acquisition-deal-called-off-9340319).

**Implied multiples (my calculations)**
- PhysicsWallah: about 10.9x FY25 revenue at the IPO price and about 15x at the day-one close.
- Drishti talks: about 6.2–7.4x revenue and 28–33x profit.
- Unacademy: about 2.0x FY25 revenue of ₹826 cr, or about 4.9x Dealroom's ₹400 cr figure.
- Utkarsh tranche: implies about ₹216 cr for 100%, roughly 1.3x FY25 turnover. Tranche pricing may not reflect true enterprise value.
- PrepLadder ($50M on 80,000 subscribers) is about $625, or roughly ₹46,000, per subscriber. This was a 2020-era price.

**Precedent**
- Narrow vertical tools are acquired at $20–50M. Billion-dollar outcomes required owning the full exam journey across many exams (PhysicsWallah's 13 categories) or premium medical niches with high ARPU (PrepLadder, Marrow).
- A river or maps tool is a feature, not a company, unless it becomes the wedge into "visual GS". That would mean expanding to all of geography, then environment, then history maps and IR maps, then full test series across UPSC, state PSCs and defence.
- The likely exit for a successful niche is acquisition by PhysicsWallah, Adda247, upGrad-Unacademy, VisionIAS or Drishti at about 1–5x revenue. PhysicsWallah, flush with IPO cash, is an active buyer in UPSC (Sarrthi stake; approaches to Drishti and Rau's).

### Gaps
- No Marrow valuation was found.
- No disclosed revenue for SuperKalam, PadhAI or EdgeUp, and no later funding rounds for SuperKalam.
- No precise valuation for Adda247 or Testbook after 2022.
- GeoGuessr financials are weakly sourced.

## 6. What B2B angles exist (coaching institutes, schools, governments, licensing)?

### Takeaway
B2B demand exists but is fragile. Classplus sold software to coaching institutes at ₹13,000–50,000 a year plus a 15% commission, saw tutors churn to free WhatsApp and YouTube after COVID, and pivoted to B2C (Testbook). The most promising B2B and B2G buyers are probably (a) the 583+ Delhi institutes and similar regional chains, for a white-label map module, (b) state free-coaching schemes such as UP's Abhyudaya, and (c) schools for CBSE Class 9–12 geography. None of these was validated with pricing evidence in this research.

### Cited Findings
- Classplus pricing: ₹13,000–50,000 a year per institute plus a 15% commission on course sales. After offline reopening, "tutors found less reason to pay for SaaS tools" and moved to free WhatsApp and YouTube. In FY24 the core SaaS business was 34.6% of ₹213 cr consolidated revenue — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/from-enabler-to-aggregator-classplus-flips-its-edtech-playbook/).
- EdgeUp (Chennai) raised ₹8 cr seed to sell AI analytics to UPSC and state-exam coaching centres in Tamil Nadu — [VentureIntelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/private-equity/test-prep-platform-edgeup-attracts-$1-m).
- Delhi had 583 coaching institutes (police submission to the Delhi High Court, 2024) — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/raus-ias-tragedy-why-rs-58000-cr-coaching-industry-needs-strict-regulations-2).
- UP Abhyudaya (since February 2021): free coaching for UPSC, UPPSC, NDA/CDS, JEE and NEET through 156 centres, more than 82,209 students supported, tablets for eligible students. Enrolment was 6,000 in 2021-22 and 25,380 in 2022-23 — [eGov](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2024/10/up-government-announces-free-coaching-support-to-700-meritorious-upsc-uppsc-aspirants/); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/amp/story/news/education/up-free-coaching-for-upsc-jee-neet-cds-nda-abhyuday-up-government-in-registration).
- School scale: about 43.7 lakh CBSE Class 10 and 12 board candidates in 2026 — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-board-exam-2026-begins-today-class-10-12-new-format-45-lakh-appear-admit-card-timings-question-paper-pdf-26-countries/amp).
- PhysicsWallah's PW Drona (teacher tools) and PW Curious Jr./K-12 lines show incumbents moving into schools. PhysicsWallah's online K-12 and early-learning revenue grew 88% to about ₹105 cr in Q1 FY27 — [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/physicswallah-reports-4-13-million-paying-users-in-fy25-diversifies-across-13-education-segments-82106.htm); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/08/physicswallah-q1-fy27-revenue-jumps-24-1054-crore-amid-88-crore-net-loss).

### Inferences
- **White-label "interactive river and map atlas" for coaching chains.** At ₹25,000–1 lakh a year per institute or branch (benchmarked on Classplus's ₹13,000–50,000 SaaS plus value-add), 500 institutes would yield about ₹1.25–5 cr a year. That is meaningful, but the churn risk shown by Classplus applies.
- **Licensing to big brands.** Licensing interactive map content to PhysicsWallah, Adda247/StudyIQ or VisionIAS is a faster route to revenue, and it doubles as an acquisition funnel.
- **Schools and government.** These are larger in head-count but need procurement and sales. State schemes (Abhyudaya and similar) buy coaching delivery, so a map module could be bundled through a coaching partner rather than sold directly.

### Gaps
- No data on prices schools pay for geography digital content, or on smart-class and atlas licensing deals (for example Extramarks or LEAD).
- No budget figure for Abhyudaya; Rajasthan's Anuprati and other state schemes were not found.
- No evidence of coaching institutes paying for white-label map modules specifically.

## 7. What are the main risks?

### Takeaway
The main risks are:
- A shrinking and saturated UPSC applicant pool.
- An edtech funding collapse: down about 94% from 2021 to 2023, with Unacademy sold 94% below peak.
- Abundant free content: brands have 10–100M YouTube subscribers, and Telegram piracy is widespread.
- Heavy seasonality around Prelims dates.
- Regulatory and platform shocks: the Telegram block, coaching crackdowns, and consumer-protection penalties.

### Cited Findings
**Demand shrinkage and saturation**
- UPSC applicants fell from about 13.4 lakh (2024) to about 9.4–9.5 lakh (2025) and 8.19 lakh (2026) — [ValidCollege](https://validcollege.com/news/result/upsc-cse-prelims-2026-sees-549-lakh-candidates-appear-answer-keys-expected-soon-m891); [Vajiram](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/how-many-students-appeared-for-upsc/).
- SSC CGL applicants fell from 36.73 lakh (2024) to about 28 lakh (2025) — [t.me/scorekhel](https://t.me/s/scorekhel); [Adda247](https://www.adda247.com/exams/ssc/ssc-received-28-lakh-applications-for-cgl-posts-against-14582-vacancies/).
- Drishti's FY25 revenue fell 10% on "sectoral normalisation after the post-COVID surge" — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/drishti-ias-posts-rs-364-cr-revenue-and-rs-61-cr-pat-in-fy25-11034727).
- Adda247 revenue was flat, with 20% layoffs and slowing StudyIQ online traction — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/adda247-lays-off-20-workforce-ahead-of-ipo/).

**Funding winter**
- Edtech funding was $4.3B in 2021 and $265M in 2023 — [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/others/indias-edtech-pivot-funding-led-expansion-gives-way-to-model-led-consolidation/).
- Unacademy was valued at $3.44B at its peak and sold for $206M — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/indias-unacademy-sells-to-rival-upgrad-for-206m-about-94-less-than-its-peak-valuation/).

**Free content and piracy**
- Brand YouTube reach: StudyIQ 20.4M, Drishti 12.8M, PhysicsWallah 98.8M, Adda247 28M (see Section 4).
- Recurring Delhi High Court piracy orders against Telegram — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/?p=270311).
- Free AI tutors (PadhAI with more than 200,000 users) are moving into explanatory content — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/04/edtech-sigiqai-9-million-seed-funding-gsv-ventures-peak-xv-ai).

**Regulation and platforms**
- Telegram blocked nationally from 16 to 22 June 2026 — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/breaking-news/telegram-blocked-india-neet-paper-leak-ban).
- Delhi coaching crackdown after the 2024 basement deaths (only 67 of 583 institutes had fire-safety certificates) — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/raus-ias-tragedy-why-rs-58000-cr-coaching-industry-needs-strict-regulations-2).
- StudyIQ reportedly fined ₹7 lakh by the CCPA for misleading advertising (December 2024; single source) — [EducationWorld](https://educationworld.in/5-youtube-channels-every-serious-upsc-aspirant-should-follow/).
- Since 2025, sharing memory-based exam questions is reportedly restricted under the 2024 Public Examinations Act, which limits PYQ-style content for SSC — [AspirantMitraa](https://www.aspirantmitraa.com/exams/ssc-cgl-tier-1-exam-analysis).

**Seasonality**
- Prelims: 16 June 2024 and 24 May 2026; results about three weeks later — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/upsc-cse-final-result-2024-out-shakti-dubey-topper-civil-services-1009-recommendations-ias-ifs-ips-central-services-upsc-gov-in); [IASGyan](https://www.iasgyan.in/blogs/upsc-prelims-result-2026-declared-13343-candidates-qualify-for-mains).
- PhysicsWallah's Q1 FY27 offline growth was hit by a shift in NEET timing, showing that exam calendars drive revenue timing — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/08/physicswallah-q1-fy27-revenue-jumps-24-1054-crore-amid-88-crore-net-loss).

### Inferences
- **Seasonality.** UPSC map and river revision demand likely peaks from January to May before Prelims, and again before Mains in August–September. Recurring revenue needs a multi-exam calendar: state PSCs, SSC and defence (NDA/CDS twice a year) fill the gaps.
- **Free-content competition.** Free YouTube map videos already exist, so a product must win on interactivity, spaced-repetition retention and PYQ linkage, not on content alone.
- **Positioning against the funding winter.** Positioning for a bootstrapped or capital-efficient path with an acquisition-ready asset is more realistic than a venture-scale B2C bet on rivers alone.

### Gaps
