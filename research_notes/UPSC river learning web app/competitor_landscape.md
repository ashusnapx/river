# Competitor Landscape: UPSC River Geography and Map-Based Learning Tools (India, as of 18 Oct 2026)

Method note: Play Store figures (downloads, rating, review count, "Updated on" date, in-app purchase range, review text) come from live listing pages fetched on 18 Oct 2026 with `hl=en_IN&gl=IN`. Play shows Indian units: 1K+ = 1,000+, 1L+ = 100,000+, 5L+ = 500,000+, 10L+ = 1M+, 50L+ = 5M+, 1Cr+ = 10M+. "IAP max" is Play's "₹X per item" ceiling, not a confirmed plan price. REA reverse-engineering tools were used on two public web pages (iasnova.com rivers atlas, learnfrommaps.com) via a local headless Chrome. No login, no paid content. Reddit could not be accessed: WebFetch is blocked for reddit.com, the JSON API returned non-JSON, the PullPush archive returned no data, and the headless browser got a "Prove your humanity" CAPTCHA, which was not bypassed.

## Q1. Which apps and websites exist for UPSC mapping, geography and river practice, and what do they offer?

### Takeaway
The field is fragmented and thin. No product combines an interactive river-network map, deep tributary coverage, PYQ linkage, spaced repetition and Hindi. Two products come closest. **Learn From Maps** is a Flutter app with an OSM-based interactive atlas and a drainage layer, 5K+ installs, updated 8 Oct 2026. **IASNOVA's "Rivers of India" atlas** is a free, ad-supported WordPress page with 48 rivers drawn as inline SVG, launched 18 Jul 2026. Download leaders are generic India map quizzes: Qbis "India Map Quiz" has 500K+ installs and "India River Map" (Da Games) has 100K+, but it covers only 4 rivers. AI tutor apps and large coaching apps have no interactive map module.

#### B. Web-based map and river resources

**IASNOVA "Rivers of India: Interactive Map"** (published 18 Jul 2026). Source: [iasnova.com](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/).
- Coverage: 48 rivers in 5 color-coded systems (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra & Barak, East-flowing, West-flowing).
- Interaction: hover or tap a river for origin, length, mouth and "the fact examiners ask". Filter by system; Index; Fullscreen.
- Content: dams (Bhakra-Nangal, Hirakud, Sardar Sarovar, Farakka and others); water disputes (Kaveri, Krishna, Mahanadi, Mullaperiyar); 7 Prelims-style MCQs; FAQ.
- Current-affairs context: e.g. the 2025 Indus Waters Treaty suspension.
- Claims a "Government of India approved political map"; "FREE ACCESS" / "Join Free".
- Sibling atlas pages: Mountain Ranges of India, Mahajanapadas, Ashokan Edicts, IVC sites, Ocean Currents, Local Winds and others. The site lists 905 articles.
- No Hindi, no PYQ tagging, no spaced repetition, no progress tracking observed.

**REA tech findings: IASNOVA** (from [the page](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/), REA `analyze_web_bundle` + `inspect_web_page`, 18 Oct 2026):
- Platform: WordPress with the Blocksy theme, WooCommerce 11.1.2, WPBakery (js_composer 7.9), the Snax plugin, MerPress (Mermaid 11.9.0), jQuery 3.7.1, Mailchimp and Contact Form 7. 35 scripts.
- Map engine: the map is a single inline `<svg>` with `viewBox`, `role` and `aria-label`. No Leaflet, Mapbox, MapLibre, canvas, GeoJSON or TopoJSON library or request was observed. This suggests a hand-built or pre-rendered SVG atlas driven by small inline scripts (four inline scripts of 0.8–7.7 KB).
- Monetization: about 30 sandboxed ad iframes and a "Grow" ad banner. A Cloudflare-style "Checking your browser" interstitial appeared on first load.

**REA tech findings: Learn From Maps web** (from [learnfrommaps.com](https://learnfrommaps.com)):
- Framework: Flutter web (`main.dart.js` 3.1 MB + `flutter_bootstrap.js`).
- App subdomains: upsc.learnfrommaps.com and play.learnfrommaps.com load Firebase 10.7.0 (app, auth, firestore), Razorpay checkout and Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise.
- Map: the hero screenshot shows an OpenStreetMap basemap ("© OpenStreetMap contributors") with colored river polylines (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra, Narmada, Tapi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri), a Godavari info popup ("Second largest river inside India…") and an "India Rivers" layer selector. No map-library string (flutter_map, mapbox, maplibre, googleapis) appeared in the bundle; the tile and library source was not resolved.

#### E. Exam demand signal (how much mapping matters)
- VisionIAS says mapping questions averaged about **10 per year** in GS Prelims, 2014–2024 — [VisionIAS Smart Mapping](http://visionias.in/smart-mapping-classes/). PW gives a different figure: **3–5 map-based questions** per year — [PW](https://www.pw.live/upsc/exams/upsc-map-based-pyq). The two sources conflict, probably because they define "map-based" differently.
- An Anantam IAS topic count for 2013–2026 shows "Drainage System" with 14 questions and "Rivers and Drainage" with 5. Geography overall had 149 questions, averaging 9.3/yr in 2013–19 and 12.0/yr in 2020–26 — [Anantam IAS](https://anantamias.com/geography-pyq-upsc-prelims/).
- Dr Anurag Singh's Unacademy series (2021) claims mapping is about 20% of GS Prelims. This is an educator's claim — [Unacademy](https://unacademy.com/course/a/0FZQNC9S).

### Inferences
- The best-distributed India map quiz is Qbis, with 500K+ installs over about 4.5 years. It is built by a solo foreign developer and monetized with $0.99–$3.99 unlocks. This suggests the "India map quiz" niche gets steady organic installs but pays little on its own.
- IASNOVA (July 2026) is the closest functional analogue to a "rivers-of-India interactive atlas". It is a content-SEO play: free, ad-funded, WordPress plus a hand-built SVG, with no accounts, progress, SRS or PYQ linking. It shows that a polished river atlas can be built cheaply, and also that nobody yet owns the learning loop around one.
- Learn From Maps is the only funded-looking, app-shaped competitor with a real basemap and river polylines, using Firebase and Razorpay. Its traction is small (5K+ installs, self-claimed 1,000+ active users) and its scope is broad (history plus geography). That breadth likely limits river depth.
- The OSM basemap used by Learn From Maps may depict Indian borders in ways that are non-compliant for Indian exam content. IASNOVA explicitly claims a GoI-approved boundary, which suggests competitors see boundary compliance as a trust signal. This was not verified legally.

## Q2. What do user reviews praise and complain about?

### Takeaway
Map-app reviewers want **more depth**: more rivers, physical features and recent data. They want **correct, consistent answers**, **real interactivity** rather than static images, **fewer ads** and **English/Hindi parity**. AI-tutor reviewers praise streaks, nudges and PYQ-plus-news bundling. Their complaints are about subscription paywalls, app instability and support. Reddit sentiment could not be collected.

  - Drishti: "videos won't run", "invalid request" at signup, "2–4 minutes to load" — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drishti.academy.app&hl=en_IN).

  - StudyIQ: course-transfer and refund disputes — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.studyiq.android&hl=en_IN).

### Inferences
- The top unmet ask in map apps is **coverage depth plus accuracy for UPSC**: more rivers, tributaries, plateaus and recent changes. A river-focused product with a verified, larger river and tributary set answers the most-repeated complaint directly.
- Ad-gated unlocks and static images are recurring sources of frustration. A clean, ad-free, truly interactive experience is a differentiator.
- Learners respond to streaks, nudges and leaderboards (SuperKalam, PrepAiro), but pushback on price is strong (PadhAI). This favors a free core with a low-priced pro tier.
- The data-consumption complaint (Prayas) is a warning for heavy map tiles. Vector or offline-first rendering matters in low-bandwidth India.

### Gaps
- Reddit r/UPSC opinions could not be retrieved (blocked fetch and a CAPTCHA that was not bypassed). Telegram and Quora sentiment were not checked.
- Review samples are the 3 "most relevant" reviews Play shows per listing, not a full corpus.

## Q4. Are there global analogues showing a narrow, visual, single-topic prep tool can get large?

### Takeaway
Yes. Narrow visual-learning tools have reached meaningful scale and exits:
- GeoGuessr/Seterra: 40M accounts; subscription; acquired Seterra in 2022.
- Complete Anatomy: acquired by Elsevier for about $50M.
- Sketchy: 30K+ paid subscribers and about $7M revenue in 2020; $30M majority stake.
- Kenhub: claims 6M+ users and 100K+ Premium.
- PrepLadder: acquired for $50M with 85K subscribers.
- Marrow: subscription revenue of about ₹461 Cr in FY23, per secondary reporting.
- Duolingo shows the micro-learning ceiling: 52.7M DAU and about $1.16B bookings in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **GeoGuessr + Seterra**:
  - GeoGuessr acquired Seterra in 2022; it had 40M player accounts by Jul 2022.
  - Free tier is limited; Pro subscription for unlimited play; "Elite" tier.
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GeoGuessr).
  - Seterra: free web, ads on mobile, premium ad-free "$1.99/month" — [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/guide/best-geoguessr-alternatives/).
  - Unverified: about $30M ARR and a $400M valuation in a partial sale to Orkila Capital — [newsletter](https://private-equity-bros.beehiiv.com/p/this-simple-app-was-bootstrapped-to-a-400m-valuation).
- **Complete Anatomy (3D4Medical)**: acquired by Elsevier (RELX) in Nov 2019 for about €46M (~US$50.6M); 1M+ registered users at 300+ universities; app downloaded 25M+ times (company claim) — [EdSurge](https://edsurge.com/news/2019-11-27-elsevier-acquires-3d-anatomy-content-provider-for-50m); [ThinkBusiness](https://www.thinkbusiness.ie/articles/irish-medtech-3d4medical-bought-for-50-6m/); [HTN](https://htn.co.uk/2019/11/28/elsevier-acquires-3d4medical/).
- **Kenhub** (Berlin, founded 2012):
  - Pricing pages claim 6.1M–6.6M users (varies by page version) and "over 100,000 students" on Premium.
  - Premium: $39/month, $29/month on a 3-month plan, or $290 lifetime.
  - Sources: [Kenhub pricing](https://www.kenhub.com/en/pricing); [Kenhub about](https://www.kenhub.com/en/about).
  - Revenue of about $3.3M is a third-party estimate only — [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/kenhub).
- **Sketchy** (visual mnemonics for medical exams): The Chernin Group took a majority stake for $30M (Sep 2020); 30K+ paid subscribers; on track for about $7M revenue in 2020; 20% of revenue from medical-school contracts — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/12/02/sketchy-medical-series-a-tcg-reach-capital). Stale (2020). A Bitscale estimate of $33.1M revenue is unverified — [Bitscale](https://bitscale.ai/directory/sketchy).
- **PrepLadder** (NEET-PG): acquired by Unacademy for $50M (Jul 2020); 85K+ active subscribers at the time — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/unacademy-acquires-edtech-startup-prepladder-for-50-mn/); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2020/07/edtech-startup-unacademy-prepladder). Stale (2020).
- **Marrow** (NEET-PG):
  - Owned by Daily Rounds / Neuroglia Health; Daily Rounds was acquired by M3 in 2019 — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/daily-rounds).
  - Reported subscription revenue of ₹461 Cr in FY23, up from ₹361 Cr; profitable — [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/story/the-most-successful-edtech-you-havent-heard-of/). This figure came via a search snippet; the paywalled article was not fetched.
  - "Over 500,000 students" — [Marrow](https://marrow.com/about-us).
- **Duolingo** (FY2025 SEC annual report): 52.7M DAU (vs 40.5M), 133.1M MAU, 12.2M paid subscribers (~9% of MAU), total bookings $1.158B, subscription bookings $996M — [SEC ARS FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026025737/duolingodecember312025an.htm); [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm).

### Inferences
- The proven patterns are: freemium plus subscription (GeoGuessr, Kenhub, Duolingo); institutional or B2B contracts (Sketchy at 20% of revenue, Complete Anatomy at universities); and acquisition by a larger content or test-prep house (Elsevier, Unacademy, M3, GeoGuessr). The Indian parallel would be acquisition by, or licensing to, a coaching brand (PW, StudyIQ/Adda247, VisionIAS) or an AI-tutor app lacking maps (SuperKalam, PadhAI).
- None of these became large on a single sub-topic. Each owns a whole visual domain (all of anatomy, all of world geography). A rivers-only wedge likely needs a path to "all UPSC mapping": rivers, then mountains and passes, protected areas, places in news, and history maps. Learn From Maps and IASNOVA are already trying that breadth.
- The UPSC market is much smaller than NEET-PG or USMLE in paying users per niche. Revenue ceilings for a pure UPSC map tool are likely in the low crores unless it expands to State PSC, SSC and school geography. Many competitor listings already target those segments (Enhancerworx, Learn From Maps, Da Games).

### Gaps
- No verified current (2025–26) figures were found for Sketchy, Kenhub revenue, Seterra's standalone usage, or Marrow beyond FY23.
- No Indian example of an acquisition of a single-topic UPSC tool was found.

## Q5. What does no existing product do well? (Gap map)

### Takeaway
Every product reviewed lacks one or more of these: a **river-system graph** (tributary hierarchy, left/right bank, confluences), **animated source-to-mouth tracing**, **PYQ-to-map-feature linking**, **spaced repetition on map items**, a **live "rivers in news" map layer**, **confusion-pair drills**, **Mains answer-writing map practice**, **full Hindi parity** and **UPSC-grade verified accuracy**. IASNOVA covers static river facts well. Enhancerworx covers bilingual drills. Learn From Maps covers basemap interactivity. Nobody combines them.

### Cited Findings
- **Tributary and river-system graph**: IASNOVA groups 48 rivers into 5 systems and notes tributary facts (e.g. Ghaghara as the Ganga's largest tributary by volume). It does not expose a navigable tributary tree or left/right-bank structure — [IASNOVA](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/). Guides tell aspirants to learn "left and right bank tributaries" manually — [Vajiram](https://vajiramandravi.com/upsc-exam/how-to-study-maps-for-upsc-ias-exam/). Qbis users ask for "100 more important rivers" — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.qbisstudio.indiaquiz&hl=en_IN).
- **Source-to-mouth trace**: IASNOVA shows origin, length and mouth on hover, with no animation — [IASNOVA](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/). Learn From Maps draws river polylines on an OSM basemap, with no animated trace observed — [learnfrommaps.com](https://learnfrommaps.com). IASNOVA's own sibling pages for the Mississippi, Nile and Amazon are "source to delta" guides, suggesting the format is wanted.
- **PYQ-to-map linking**: SuperKalam's map-PYQ compilation (2015–2025) is a static list — [SuperKalam](https://superkalam.com/upsc-preparation/resources/upsc-map-based-pyqs-2015-2025-practice-map-questions). Learn From Maps lists PYQs as a separate quiz bank — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.learnfrommaps.android&hl=en_IN). IASNOVA has 7 generic MCQs, not PYQs — [IASNOVA](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/). PYQ apps (InstaPYQ with 28,000+ PYQs, PadhAI with semantic PYQ search) have no map — [InstaPYQ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.instapyq&hl=en_IN); [PadhAI](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ajeei.padhai&hl=en_IN).
- **Spaced repetition**: No map product states SRS.
  - Enhancerworx has mistake review and streaks — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.enhancerworx.indiamap&hl=en_IN).
  - Maps Quiz has flashcards — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kiranpalsingh.mapsquiz&hl=en_IN).
  - Qbis has a Training mode — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1620314036).
  - CrackitToday has text flashcards, and users complain about their organization — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.crackittoday.upsc&hl=en_IN).
- **Rivers-in-news layer**: Today this exists only as text or PDF lists (PMF, PW, Anantam monthly "Location in News" with Wikipedia locator maps) — [PMF](https://www.pmfias.com/world-rivers-and-lakes-in-news/); [PW](https://www.pw.live/upsc/exams/rivers-and-lakes-in-news-for-upsc-2026); [Anantam](https://anantamias.com/current-affairs/location-in-news-may-2026/?pdf=1). Learn From Maps has a generic "Current Affairs" section — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.learnfrommaps.android&hl=en_IN). IASNOVA embeds news context per river (e.g. Indus Waters Treaty suspension), but not as a dated, filterable layer — [IASNOVA](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/).
- **Confusion-pair drills** (e.g. Tons vs Chambal, Gandak vs Ghaghara): no product found. The Da Games review shows learners hitting exactly this confusion, made worse by inconsistent answers — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=india.river.map.gk&hl=en_IN).
- **Mains answer-writing maps**: only guidance articles exist (Anantam: draw maps at about one-third page width, use about 8 blank India outlines, one thematic layer per sheet) plus expensive optional courses (IASgyan ₹35,400) — [Anantam](https://anantamias.com/upsc-answer-writing-diagrams/); [IASgyan](https://www.iasgyan.in/courses/upsc/geography-optional-programme). AI Mains evaluators (SuperKalam, PadhAI, PrepAiro, Prayas) evaluate text answers; none mentions map or diagram drawing practice — Play listings above.
- **Hindi**: Full bilingual parity exists only in Enhancerworx (a general India map app) — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.enhancerworx.indiamap&hl=en_IN). Abhi Ruk is Hindi-only and its users request English — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.indiariver.map&hl=en_IN). IASNOVA and Learn From Maps state no Hindi. PrepAiro supports Hindi voice but has no maps — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.prepairo.app&hl=en_IN).
- **Accuracy and boundaries**: IASNOVA claims a GoI-approved boundary and carries an FAQ, "Does this map show the correct boundaries of India?" — [IASNOVA](https://iasnova.com/interactive-atlas/rivers-of-india-interactive-map/). Enhancerworx disclaims: "not official government maps" — [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.enhancerworx.indiamap&hl=en_IN). Learn From Maps uses an OSM basemap — [learnfrommaps.com](https://learnfrommaps.com).
- **Authoritative open data exists** but is not learner-facing: India-WRIS WebGIS has river, basin, dam and canal layers with free downloads — [CWC](https://cwc.gov.in/en/water-resources-information-system-wris); [journalsofindia](https://journalsofindia.com/indian-water-resources-information-system/?print=pdf).

### Inferences
- The most defensible wedge combines a **verified river graph built from India-WRIS-grade data** (hierarchy, bank side, confluence, states crossed, dams, disputes) with **active-recall drills on that graph**:
  - drills: trace the course, pick the tributary on the correct bank, order the rivers north to south, tell confusion pairs apart;
  - **SRS scheduling** of those drills;
  - **every PYQ tagged to map features**, so a missed item resurfaces with its PYQ.

  No competitor has this loop. IASNOVA proves demand for the static layer, and the big apps prove demand for PYQs and streaks.
- A dated "rivers and places in news" layer is cheap to maintain monthly and competes with free PDFs on usability rather than content. It is also a retention hook, since it changes every month.
- Hindi parity plus offline or low-data vector rendering addresses two documented complaints at once: language mismatch and data consumption.
- Main competitive threats:
  - IASNOVA adding accounts, progress and PYQs to its existing SVG atlases.
  - Learn From Maps deepening its river layer.
  - Funded AI tutors (SigIQ $9.5M, SuperKalam ~$4M) adding a map module. Each of these could also be a partner or acquirer.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence of willingness to pay specifically for a river or map tool: no survey, no conversion data.
- Whether any coaching platform offers in-app map drills behind login was not verified.
- Mains map-drawing evaluation (photo upload of a hand-drawn map) was not found anywhere, but closed apps were not exhaustively checked.
